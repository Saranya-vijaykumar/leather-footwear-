Private Label Customization

Bespoke Branding & Finishing

Every silhouette in our catalog can be branded and tailored to your house aesthetic. Choose from our luxury customization suite.

Gold Foil & Blind Deboss

Precision hot-stamped logos on full leather heel socks, sockliners, and sole waists using 24K gold foil or deep blind debossing.

Brass Nail Sole Art

Custom geometric brass nail patterns driven into leather heel breasts and toe tips for signature brand identification and grip reinforcement.

Closed Channel Stitching

Master cordwainers cut a delicate 1mm leather flap in the outsole, sew the welt stitch, and cement the flap down for an invisible glass finish.

Custom Rigid Packaging

FSC-certified rigid drawer shoeboxes, embossed velvet travel dustbags, branded brass shoe horns, and spare waxed cotton laces.
