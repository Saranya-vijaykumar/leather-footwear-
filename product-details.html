Technical Dossier

SPEC-ARCHIVE

Our 4-Stage Atelier Process

1. Leather Grading & Clicking

Individual hides inspected under raking light; skilled clickers cut along the tightest spine grains to guarantee structural balance.

2. Lasting & Archival Resting

Uppers are pulled taut over hornbeam lasts and conditioned in humidity-controlled rooms for 72 hours to permanently memorize foot contours.

3. Goodyear 360° Welting & Cork Filling

Inseam sewn with high-tensile waxed hemp thread. The cavity is packed with granulated oak cork for natural thermal insulation and arch conformity.

4. Hand Edge Ironing & Carnauba Glazing

Soles are hand-beveled, burnished with heated copper irons, and finished with bone-rubbed organic carnauba wax to a deep mirror luster.
