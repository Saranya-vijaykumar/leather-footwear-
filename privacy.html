Privacy & Regulatory Standards

Proprietary shoe last copyright protection, strict non-disclosure agreement governance, GDPR transparency, and international customs documentation.

1. Corporate Data Controller

Vernon & Vale Atelier Footwear Manufacturers S.p.A. (registered under Italian Trade Register No. FI-928174, with operating atelier hubs in Florence, Italy and London Mayfair, UK) is the designated Data Controller regarding personal information, B2B wholesale inquiries, and technical manufacturing specifications gathered through this website.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our B2B manufacturing portal, sample request modal, or newsletter dispatch, we may collect:

  • Representative Name, Corporate Brand Identity, and Job Title.
  • Corporate Work Email Address and WhatsApp / International Phone Numbers.
  • Export Destination Country, Target Footwear Categories, and Order Quantities.
  • Proprietary CAD Last files, Tech Packs, and Insole debossing files submitted for private label quotations.

3. Strict B2B Non-Disclosure & Proprietary Last Guarantee

We operate under strict industrial secrecy. When a commercial client commissions custom lasts, proprietary brogue perforations, or branded packaging dies from Vernon & Vale:

Proprietary Last Guarantee:

Any last geometry, monogram emboss tooling, or proprietary shoe pattern developed under contract remains the sole intellectual property of the commissioning client. Under zero circumstance will such designs be licensed, exhibited, or shared with third-party competitors.

4. Use of LocalStorage & Essential Cookies

This website does not deploy predatory third-party advertising trackers or commercial remarketing pixels. We use browser localStorage exclusively to persist your user interface preferences:

  • Theme Preference (vv_theme): Remembers whether you selected Ivory Light or Dark Mode.
  • Layout Direction (vv_dir): Remembers whether you chose Left-to-Right (LTR) or Right-to-Left (RTL) reading view.

5. GDPR Compliance & Data Subject Rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK Data Protection Act 2018, you possess the right to access, rectify, export, or permanently erase your corporate representative data from our CRM records at any time with zero penalty.

6. Data Protection Officer Contact

For privacy inquiries, NDA requests, or data deletion notices, contact our Legal Counsel directly at:
Data Protection Desk: governance@vernonvale-craft.com
Vernon & Vale Legal Department, 14 Savile Row, Mayfair, London W1S 3JN, UK
