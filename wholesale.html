Worldwide Export Customs & Compliance

Export Logistics, Bonded Customs Clearance & DDP Delivery

Exporting luxury leather footwear demands rigorous adherence to international trade statutes, animal hide provenance laws, and harmonized customs tariffs.

Incoterms Flexibility: We handle EXW, FOB (Genoa / Felixstowe / Nhava Sheva), CIF, and fully delivered DDP straight to your regional hubs.

REACH & CITES Compliance: Raw calfskins, goat linings, and shell cordovans are supported with authentic veterinary and origin certificates to clear customs with zero delays.

Moisture-Proof Maritime Crating: Heavy-duty, moisture-sealed maritime export crates with humidity-absorbing desiccant sachets ensuring zero mildew or mold during ocean transit.

Protocol & Milestones

6-Stage OEM/ODM Engineering Workflow

From preliminary tech packs to bonded international customs port delivery, our disciplined European protocol eliminates production risk.

01

Tech Pack & Last Profiling

Evaluation of brand CAD schematics, dimensional instep metrics, and selection between our 82 Chisel or 73 Round lasts.

02

Tannery Leather Sourcing

Direct allocation of gold-rated French boxcalf or Tuscan vegetable crust hides with custom color strike-off approvals.

03

Precision Prototype Lasting

Bench sample fabrication within 10 business days for wear-testing, flex verification, and brand executive sign-off.

04

Full Batch Goodyear Welting

Mechanical and hand welting on our Florence production carousel with granulated cork filling and steel shank insertion.

05

AQL 1.0 Quality Control

Every pair undergoes rigorous raking-light visual inspection, stitch count validation, and European heel torque testing.

06

Custom Packaging & Bonded Air/Sea

Branded rigid boxes, anti-mildew silica wrapping, and DDP bonded freight clearance directly to your regional fulfillment hubs.
