Institutional Insights

Bulk Footwear Procurement FAQ

What is the minimum commercial order quantity (MOQ) for custom private label production?

Our flexible Tier 1 entry run starts at just 25 pairs per model across assorted sizes (EU 39–46 / US 6–13). This allows emerging luxury houses and bespoke boutiques to launch capsule collections with zero excess inventory waste. Larger scale runs of 100 to 2,000+ pairs access significant volume tier discounts.

How does Vernon & Vale protect our proprietary shoe lasts and design copyright?

Every B2B collaboration is protected under a strict bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) governed under Italian and International Commercial Law. Your customized lasts are numbered, securely archived in our private vault, and never shared or repurposed for any other brand.

What is the turnaround time for benchmark evaluation samples?

Benchmark prototypes are dispatched within 14 calendar days from technical blueprint sign-off. We ship evaluation pairs via express air courier directly to your design studio for wear-testing, leather patina evaluation, and fit approval before batch production begins.

What international certifications accompany our bulk shipments?

Every production crate includes certified Leather Working Group (LWG) Gold environmental audit certificates, EU REACH chemical compliance declarations, and authenticated Certificates of Origin verifying authentic Tuscan cordwaining origin.
