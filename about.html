Masters of the Cordwainer’s Art

Half a century of artisanal leather footwear manufacturing in the hills of Tuscany. Preserving anatomical lasts, Goodyear welting, and ethical vegetable tanning for global luxury houses.

Manufacturing Heritage, Step by Step

Vernon & Vale was founded in 1974 by master cordwainer Alistair Vernon and industrial lastmaker Marco Vale, who recognized that mass production had abandoned the enduring structural beauty of genuine Goodyear welting. What began as a single-bench workshop in Florence has grown into an international manufacturing atelier exporting over 120,000 pairs annually across 42 nations.

Our benchmarks remain uncompromising: 100% full-grain Tuscan vegetable-tanned shoulder, French Le Puy boxcalf, oak-bark pit-tanned outsoles, and 72-hour dwell lasting on carved wooden lasts.

Our Mission

To champion authentic cordwaining traditions through sustainable industrial scale and honest European bench quality.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the gold standard for luxury private label and bespoke leather footwear manufacturing.

Environmental Stewardship

Crafted to Harmonize with Nature
